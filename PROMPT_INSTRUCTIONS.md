# System Prompt Instructions for AI Bot

Hey! I need you to write a **system prompt** for our Discord AI bot. Here's what you need to know:

## What is a System Prompt?
It's the instruction that tells the AI how to behave, what personality to have, and how to respond to users.

## Important Rules for GPT-5-nano:
- **Keep responses SHORT** - Max 1-2 sentences
- **Be concise and direct** - No long explanations
- **No bullet lists** - Just plain text responses
- **No complex formatting** - Keep it simple

## What to Include in Your Prompt:

1. **Personality/Tone**:
   - Friendly? Professional? Casual? Funny?
   - Example: "You are a friendly and helpful assistant"

2. **Response Style**:
   - How long should responses be?
   - What language to use?
   - Example: "Keep responses to 1-2 sentences max"

3. **Behavior Rules**:
   - What should the bot do or NOT do?
   - Example: "Don't use bullet points or complex formatting"

4. **Special Instructions** (if needed):
   - Any specific knowledge or context?
   - Example: "You help users with Discord server management"

## Examples of GOOD System Prompts:

**Example 1 - Casual Helper:**
```
You are a friendly Discord assistant. Keep responses to 1-2 sentences max. Be direct and helpful. No bullet lists or complex formatting.
```

**Example 2 - Professional:**
```
You are a professional Discord support bot. Provide concise, accurate answers in 1-2 sentences. Stay professional and friendly.
```

**Example 3 - Funny Bot:**
```
You are a witty Discord bot with a sense of humor. Keep responses short (1-2 sentences) and add occasional jokes. No long explanations.
```

## What to AVOID:

❌ Don't make it too long (GPT-5-nano is simple)
❌ Don't ask the AI to use complex formatting
❌ Don't include contradictory instructions
❌ Don't make it too vague

## Your Task:

Write a system prompt that:
- Is **2-4 sentences max**
- Defines the **personality/tone**
- Sets **clear response guidelines**
- Is written in **English**

Just send me the prompt text when you're done!
